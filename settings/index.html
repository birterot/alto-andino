Which shortcuts Home shows under “Log a brew”, and in which order. They are also the tabs at the top of those pages. Everything stays in the menu (☰) either way.

Caffeine on Home

Home estimates the caffeine in you from the cups that were for you. It is a rough average with a range: coffee varies a lot (espresso most), and everyone is affected differently.

What to count

“Upper end” is the safety net: it counts the top of the range, so you rather overestimate.

Each day

“Carry over” counts last night's coffee as it wears off. “Reset at midnight” starts every day at zero.

Your sleep line

With a bedtime, Home shows how much caffeine will be left when you go to bed, and by when your last coffee should be, so it is under your sleep line by then.

How it is worked out →

Off: Home shows no caffeine. Your cups are still logged as usual, and you can turn it on again here any time.

Sleep check

On: in the morning, Home asks how you slept (badly, okay or well), one tap. After a few good and a few bad nights, it compares them with your coffee and gives a tip, for example to have your last coffee earlier. Only you see your answers.
