Caffeine

How the caffeine on Home is worked out, and how sure it can be.

The idea

Caffeine in the cup = grams of coffee × caffeine in the beans × how much of it the method pulls out

Then, in your body, it rises for about 45 minutes and halves roughly every 5 hours. That is the curve on Home. A cup counts for everyone it was for (shared, if several), at the time they drank it (or the time of the brew).

Taken from the coffee on your shelf: the Decaf box, and Robusta in the variety (with another variety too, it counts as a blend). Roasting changes little: darker beans lost more water and mass, so a gram of them has a few per cent more caffeine (we count light 0.98, medium 1, dark 1.04).

How much the method pulls out

Caffeine dissolves easily, much more easily than most of the flavour, so the methods differ less than you might think.

MethodPulled out
Filter (V60, Chemex, Kalita, drip machine)about 95 %
Immersion (French press, AeroPress, Clever)about 90 %
Moka pot, cold brewabout 85 %
Espresso, ristretto (1:1)about 65 %
Espresso, normal (1:2)about 75 %
Espresso, lungo (1:3)about 85 %

For espresso the yield you logged decides it (between 60 and 90 %). The method comes from the device's name.

How sure is it?

Not very, and that is normal. Beans of the same kind differ by about a quarter either way, and espresso differs most: in a study of espressos bought in cafes in Glasgow (2012), one cup had anything from about 50 to over 300 mg. So Home shows an estimate with a range: 25 % either way, 30 % for espresso. In Settings you can count the upper end instead, as a safety net.

Why the 50 mg line

50 mg is about half an espresso. Below it, the caffeine left is unlikely to keep most people awake, so on the curve it is a rough guide for when an evening is sleep-friendly. It is not a medical limit: people break caffeine down at very different speeds (the 5 hours can be anything from about 2 to 10), and if you are sensitive, aim lower.

How much is a lot?

For healthy adults, the European Food Safety Authority considers up to about 400 mg a day, and up to about 200 mg at once, not a concern. During pregnancy the advice is up to about 200 mg a day. Home mentions it when a day goes over 400 mg.
