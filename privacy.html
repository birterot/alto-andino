Privacy

What Alto Andino stores about you, why, who helps us run it, and what you can do about it.

As of October 2026

Who is responsible

Alto Andino is a private, non-commercial project by Birte and Andrés. Questions, or anything about your data: hello@alto-andino.com.

The short version

  • We store what you put into the app, so that it works: your account, your coffees and brews, your ratings, and the settings you choose.
  • Your household sees your household's coffees and brews. Friends only see what you choose to share (fun stats, favourites, taste profile, your coffees with their best recipe), your profiles, and a cup you make for them (so they can rate it). Nobody else sees anything.
  • No ads, no tracking by other companies, no selling of data. The app only counts which of its parts get opened (each once a day per phone, without your name), to see what to improve. No cookie banner, because nothing is stored on your phone that is not needed for the app itself.
  • You can download everything, delete parts of your data (and keep your account), or delete your account at any time, in Settings, under "Your data".

The legal basis is that you use the app and we provide it to you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for keeping the sign-in safe from bots and for counting which parts of the app are used, our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)); for the sleep answers, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time by turning the Sleep check off and deleting the answers.

On your phone

The app keeps a few things in your browser's storage so it is fast and remembers you: your sign-in, the last view of your shelf, unsaved brews, some settings, and which parts you opened today (so each counts only once). They never leave your phone except as described here. Signing out clears your shelf from the phone.

How long

As long as you have an account. When you delete it, your account and everything that is only yours goes straight away. In a household you share, the coffees and brews stay for the others, without your name. Backups at our database provider are kept for a short time and then overwritten.

Your rights

You can see, correct, download and delete your data (Settings, "Your data"), limit or object to how we use it, and withdraw your consent for the sleep answers at any time. For anything else, write to hello@alto-andino.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority.
